I teach AI the way I wish someone had taught me ~ **plainly**.

After 17 years in the VA world, I now teach Claude to the people I came from: non-technical solopreneurs, coaches, and virtual assistants who keep hearing "just use AI" with nobody explaining the how.

My one rule: AI should never feel like one more thing you have to figure out. So I break it down step by step until it fits the way you already work.

☕ building in public, between cities<br/>
🎀 workflows that bend around your business, not the other way around<br/>
🤍 no jargon, no twenty tabs open at midnight
